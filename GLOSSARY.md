# MyBest

A personal Android app that helps one person break the weekend chain of going out, drinking and overspending, using money as the motivator, while tracking gym, diet, English and alcohol as daily checks.

## Weekend

**Outing**:
A night out, recorded either as planned (through the pre-outing screen) or as unplanned (reported at the next-day check-in).
_Avoid_: Party, night, event

**Limits**:
The three caps set before a planned outing: money, drinks and return time.
_Avoid_: Budget, rules, caps

**Pre-outing checklist**:
The two steps confirmed before going out: credit cards off and out of the phone wallet, outing account loaded with the money limit.
_Avoid_: Preparation

**Won night**:
A Friday or Saturday evening where the user chose not to go out.
_Avoid_: Saved night, skipped outing

**Check-in**:
The next-morning record of an outing: total spent, limits kept or exceeded, and mood.
_Avoid_: Review, log, journal

## Consistency

**Daily check**:
A yes/no mark for one day on gym, diet, English or alcohol.
_Avoid_: Habit, toggle, task

**Weekly target**:
The number of days per week a daily check should be done, set by the user.
_Avoid_: Goal (reserved for money and weight)

**Relapse**:
An outing that broke a limit, exceeded the monthly outing limit, or was unplanned.
_Avoid_: Failure, slip, fall

**Return**:
The first day after a relapse with diet done and no alcohol.
_Avoid_: Recovery, comeback

**Return time**:
Days from a relapse to its return.
_Avoid_: Streak

**App day**:
The 24 hours from 05:00 to 05:00 that every record belongs to.
_Avoid_: Calendar day, date

## Money

**Debt**:
A loan or credit with a balance, a monthly installment and a payment day.
_Avoid_: Loan, credit, letra

**Installment**:
The monthly amount due on a debt.
_Avoid_: Fee, quota, cuota

**Purchase goal**:
One durable item the user wants, with a price, funded only by real contributions.
_Avoid_: Savings goal, wish

**Estimated outing cost**:
The expected spend of one outing: the declared typical cost until three outings exist, then their average.
_Avoid_: Average night

**Not spent (estimated)**:
The monthly sum of estimated outing costs from won nights; never real money.
_Avoid_: Savings

## Body

**Weigh-in**:
The weekly weight record on the user-chosen day.
_Avoid_: Weight entry
